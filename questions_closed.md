# Closed questions — ai-project-template

Answered questions, moved here from [questions.md](questions.md). Newest first.

**Every entry records what changed as a result** — the commit, the decision id, the ticket that
unblocked. That field is the point of the file. A closed question with no consequence recorded
is indistinguishable from one that was answered and then forgotten anyway, and it is exactly the
one somebody re-asks six weeks later.

Entries are **append-only**. If an answer is later reversed, that is a new question and a new
entry saying so — never an edit to the old one.

Format:

```markdown
## PRJ-Q<n> — <the question, in one line>

**Asked** YYYY-MM-DD · **Answered** YYYY-MM-DD by <who>
**Answer.** <what they decided, in their terms>
**Blocked.** <the ticket ids that were waiting>
**What changed.** <commit sha, decision id, what moved from ❓ to ⬜/🟡>
```
